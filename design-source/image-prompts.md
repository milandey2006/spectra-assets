# Spectra Assets: image prompts

Drop the generated images into `public/images/solutions/` with **exactly** these file names
(JPG, **7:5 landscape**, about 1680×1200, under ~300 KB each). The site picks them up automatically;
any image that is missing shows a light placeholder, so they can be added one at a time.

## Shared style (add to the end of every prompt)

> Modern corporate editorial photograph. Minimalist contemporary interiors and architecture,
> glass, steel, marble and warm wood, soft natural light, muted neutral palette with subtle
> teal accents, shallow depth of field, shot on a 35mm lens, premium private-banking mood,
> calm and trustworthy. **No faces visible**: people appear only from behind, as silhouettes,
> cropped at the shoulders, or as hands. No text, no logos, no watermarks. Aspect ratio 7:5.

## What we believe — principles (N26-style photo cards, 4:5 portrait)

Drop the generated images into `public/images/principles/` with these exact
file names. **Portrait 4:5 crop, about 1200x1500**, under ~350 KB each. The
site auto-detects them; a missing image shows a dark placeholder tile.

**N26 reference:** each card is a real, warm, cinematic photograph — a
person in an environment (never a stock-y closeup), rich golden-hour light
or clean daylight, deep colour, subtle green/teal tones in the scene where
possible. Composition leaves room on one side (the card is portrait), and
the subject is often looking away from the camera. **No faces looking
straight at the lens.** No text, no logos, no UI overlays baked in.

**Shared style suffix (add to every prompt):**

> Editorial commercial photograph, cinematic natural light, warm golden
> hour or soft afternoon sun, shallow depth of field, shot on a 35mm lens,
> deep colour grade, calm confident premium mood, aspect ratio 4:5,
> ultra-realistic, high resolution. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

### How to generate these (recommended)
Any modern image model works, but for best consistency use **Midjourney v6
/ v7** or **Google's Imagen 3** with the shared style suffix appended:

1. Paste the prompt above + the shared suffix.
2. Ask for **portrait 4:5** (Midjourney: `--ar 4:5 --style raw`).
3. Pick the frame where the subject is **not looking at camera**.
4. Save as JPG at ~1200x1500, quality ~80. Compress with tinypng.com if
   over ~350 KB. Drop into `public/images/principles/` with the file
   name above. The site picks it up on next reload.
